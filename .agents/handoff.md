# Workstream Handoff Template

Prepare a BetterMTA workstream handoff containing only:

1. What was implemented
2. Files changed
3. Public interfaces and schemas
4. Assumptions
5. Validation commands
6. Validation results
7. Fixture or sample-data instructions
8. Known defects
9. Known limitations
10. Decisions requiring conductor approval
11. Exact next integration step

Clearly distinguish implemented, tested, mocked, deferred, and blocked. Do not include speculative completion claims.
