# BetterMTA public-beta limitations copy

**Draft — not approved or published.** This copy must be reviewed and placed in
the public product before a public-beta status decision.

BetterMTA is an NYC subway-first route planner. It is designed to rank subway
options around the lines you choose; it is not a complete regional trip
planner and does not currently promise bus, commuter rail, PATH, ferry,
driving, cycling, or native-app coverage.

Routes depend on the available static schedule, realtime feed, and candidate
set. BetterMTA shows whether information is live, stale, schedule-only,
synthetic, or unavailable. When realtime is stale or unavailable, times and
service conditions may be less accurate. A missing route does not prove that
no possible trip exists.

Preferred lines are used when feasible within the candidates BetterMTA can
validate. When all selected lines cannot be used, the product ranks routes
using the maximum feasible subset it found and names omitted preferences.
Walking, transfers, service changes, station access, and elevator conditions
can change; confirm critical accessibility needs and urgent service conditions
with official MTA information.

No account is required for the public beta. BetterMTA must not retain precise
trip locations or preference history beyond the approved privacy/consent
model. Address/place search remains disabled until its provider, attribution,
privacy, and operating gates are approved.

BetterMTA does not claim to beat Google Maps, Apple Maps, Citymapper, the MTA,
or any other product. Comparative statements require a published methodology
and reproducible corpus evidence. BetterMTA is not affiliated with or endorsed
by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority.

Do not use BetterMTA as an emergency service. Follow station staff, official
alerts, posted signs, and emergency instructions when they conflict with an
app result.
