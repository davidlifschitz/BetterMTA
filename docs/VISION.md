# BetterMTA Vision

BetterMTA is the transit planner for people who actually know the NYC subway.

Mainstream navigation tools decide the route and ask the rider to accept it. BetterMTA lets the rider supply local knowledge—especially which lines they want to use—and then independently computes and compares the fastest valid alternatives.

## Promise
You know the subway. BetterMTA listens, checks, and shows the tradeoffs.

## First user
A daily NYC commuter or transit power user who already has opinions about lines, transfers, and service patterns.

## Differentiator
- Familiar Google-like trip entry.
- Line selection before or after route search.
- Required-line constrained routing.
- Top three alternatives.
- Graceful partial matches when all selected lines cannot form a sensible trip.
- Side-by-side evidence: time, walking, waiting, transfers, reliability, and crowding.

## North-star outcome
Users choose BetterMTA for recurring trips because it identifies a faster or more suitable route that a default planner did not present.
